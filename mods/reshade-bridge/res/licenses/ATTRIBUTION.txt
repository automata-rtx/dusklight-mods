ReShade Bridge for Dusklight - provenance
=========================================

This release has two parts: the Dusklight mod (reshade_bridge.dusk) and a
ReShade add-on (dusklight_reshade_bridge.addon64). Both are this project's own
code. The add-on is compiled against two sets of third-party headers:

ReShade add-on API headers
--------------------------
reshade.hpp, reshade_api*.hpp, reshade_events.hpp and reshade_overlay.hpp from
ReShade (https://github.com/crosire/reshade, tag v6.8.0), copyright 2014
Patrick Mours, available under the BSD 3-Clause license (reproduced in
RESHADE-BSD-3.txt) or the MIT license, at the user's option.

Dear ImGui headers
------------------
imgui.h and imconfig.h from Dear ImGui 1.92.5
(https://github.com/ocornut/imgui), copyright 2014-2025 Omar Cornut, under the
MIT license (reproduced in IMGUI-MIT.txt). The add-on draws its overlay tab
through ReShade's own copy of Dear ImGui; no ImGui code is compiled into it.

This project is not made by or affiliated with the ReShade project. ReShade
itself and ReShade effects are not included; users install their own.
